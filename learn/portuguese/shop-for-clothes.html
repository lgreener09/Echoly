How to shop for clothes in Portuguese

Sizes, colors, fitting rooms and the all-important "I'll take it". Here's everything you need to shop for clothes without pointing and hoping for the best.

Key Portuguese phrases

PortugueseEnglishListen
Só estou dando uma olhada, obrigado. I'm just looking, thanks.
Tem esse no tamanho M? Do you have this in a medium?
Tem em outra cor? Do you have it in another color?
Onde ficam os provadores? Where are the fitting rooms?
Posso experimentar? Can I try it on?
Está grande demais / pequeno demais. It's too big / too small.
Ficou bom. It fits well.
Quanto custa? How much does it cost?
Está em promoção? Is it on sale?
Vou levar. I'll take it.

Example conversation

Shop assistant
Oi! Posso ajudar? Hello! Can I help you?
You
Sim, tem essa camisa no tamanho M? Yes, do you have this shirt in a medium?
Shop assistant
Vou ver... Aqui está. Let me check... Here you go.
You
Obrigado. Onde ficam os provadores? Thanks. Where are the fitting rooms?
Shop assistant
Lá no fundo, à esquerda. At the back, on the left.
You
Ficou bom. Vou levar! It fits well. I'll take it!

Good to know

💡 Brazilians often say "ficou bom" (it looks good, it fits) after trying something on. "Provador" is the fitting room, and many shops let you pay in installments ("parcelado").
