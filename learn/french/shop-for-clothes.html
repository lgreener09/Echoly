How to shop for clothes in French

Sizes, colors, fitting rooms and the all-important "I'll take it". Here's everything you need to shop for clothes without pointing and hoping for the best.

Practice this conversation out loud, free →

Key French phrases

FrenchEnglishListen
Je regarde seulement, merci. I'm just looking, thanks.
Vous l'avez en M ? Do you have this in a medium?
Vous l'avez dans une autre couleur ? Do you have it in another color?
Où sont les cabines d'essayage ? Where are the fitting rooms?
Je peux l'essayer ? Can I try it on?
C'est trop grand / trop petit. It's too big / too small.
Ça me va bien. It fits well.
Combien ça coûte ? How much does it cost?
C'est en solde ? Is it on sale?
Je le prends. I'll take it.

Example conversation

Shop assistant
Bonjour ! Je peux vous aider ? Hello! Can I help you?
You
Oui, vous avez cette chemise en M ? Yes, do you have this shirt in a medium?
Shop assistant
Je vais voir... Voilà. Let me check... Here you go.
You
Merci. Où sont les cabines d'essayage ? Thanks. Where are the fitting rooms?
Shop assistant
Au fond, à gauche. At the back, on the left.
You
Elle me va bien. Je la prends ! It fits well. I'll take it!

Good to know

💡 "Ça me va" means it fits or suits you. In France, the big sales ("les soldes") happen at fixed times, usually in January and early summer.
