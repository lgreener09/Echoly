How to order coffee in French

Ordering a coffee is one of the first real conversations you'll have abroad. It's short and predictable, and a great place to build confidence. Here are the phrases you'll actually hear and say at the counter.

Key French phrases

FrenchEnglishListen
Bonjour Hello / Good morning
Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît. I'd like a coffee, please.
Un café crème, s'il vous plaît. A milky coffee (latte), please.
petit / moyen / grand Small / medium / large
Sur place ou à emporter ? For here or to go?
À emporter, s'il vous plaît. To go, please.
Avec du lait d'avoine, s'il vous plaît. With oat milk, please.
C'est combien ? How much is it?
Je peux payer par carte ? Can I pay by card?
Merci, bonne journée ! Thanks, have a nice day!

Example conversation

Barista
Bonjour ! Qu'est-ce que je vous sers ? Hi! What can I get you?
You
Je voudrais un café crème, s'il vous plaît. I'd like a latte, please.
Barista
Petit ou grand ? Small or large?
You
Grand, à emporter. Large, to go.
Barista
Ça fait quatre euros cinquante. That's four euros fifty.
You
Voilà. Merci ! Here you go. Thanks!

Good to know

💡 In France, "un café" means a small espresso, so ask for "un café crème" if you want milk. Always say "Bonjour" before you order. Skipping it comes across as rude.
