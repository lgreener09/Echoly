How to make small talk in French

Small talk is where real fluency shows: no script, no menu, just a friendly chat. These phrases (in the casual "you" you'd use with a friendly neighbour) get you through the weather, the weekend and a warm goodbye.

Key French phrases

FrenchEnglishListen
Ça va ? How's it going?
Il fait beau aujourd'hui, hein ? Nice weather today, isn't it?
Il fait tellement chaud / froid ! It's so hot / so cold!
Tu as des projets pour le week-end ? Any plans for the weekend?
Pas grand-chose, je vais me reposer. Not much, just relaxing.
Tu as passé un bon week-end ? How was your weekend?
Tu habites ici depuis combien de temps ? How long have you lived here?
Tu fais quoi dans la vie ? What do you do for work?
Ça m'a fait plaisir de discuter ! Nice talking to you!
À la prochaine ! See you around!

Example conversation

Neighbour
Salut ! Ça va ? Hi! How's it going?
You
Ça va, merci ! Il fait beau aujourd'hui, hein ? Good, thanks! Nice weather today, isn't it?
Neighbour
Enfin ! Tu as des projets pour le week-end ? Finally! Any plans for the weekend?
You
Je vais à la plage avec des amis. Et toi ? I'm going to the beach with some friends. And you?
Neighbour
Rien de spécial, je vais me reposer à la maison. Nothing special, just relaxing at home.
You
Bonne idée ! À la prochaine ! Sounds good! See you around!

Good to know

💡 These phrases use the friendly "tu". With an older neighbour or someone you've just met, switch to "vous": "Vous avez des projets pour le week-end ?".
