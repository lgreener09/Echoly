🗼 French for your trip

French for Paris: the phrases you'll actually use

Going to Paris? Starting with a little French, even just “Bonjour”, changes how people respond to you. These are the phrases you'll actually use in cafés, shops and on the metro.

🆘 Help

FrenchEnglishListen

Merci, bonne journée ! Thanks, have a nice day!

Good to know in Paris

💡 Always say “Bonjour” when you walk into a shop or café. Skipping it can come across as rude.

💡 “Un café” is an espresso. Ask for “une carafe d'eau” for free tap water at restaurants.

💡 Service is included in restaurant prices, so tipping is optional. Rounding up or leaving a euro or two is plenty.
