How to make small talk in German

Small talk is where real fluency shows: no script, no menu, just a friendly chat. These phrases (in the casual "you" you'd use with a friendly neighbour) get you through the weather, the weekend and a warm goodbye.

Key German phrases

GermanEnglishListen
Wie geht's? How's it going?
Schönes Wetter heute, oder? Nice weather today, isn't it?
Ist das heiß / kalt heute! It's so hot / so cold!
Hast du was vor am Wochenende? Any plans for the weekend?
Nicht viel, einfach entspannen. Not much, just relaxing.
Wie war dein Wochenende? How was your weekend?
Wie lange wohnst du schon hier? How long have you lived here?
Was machst du beruflich? What do you do for work?
Schön, mit dir zu plaudern! Nice talking to you!
Bis bald! See you around!

Example conversation

Neighbour
Hallo! Wie geht's? Hi! How's it going?
You
Gut, danke! Schönes Wetter heute, oder? Good, thanks! Nice weather today, isn't it?
Neighbour
Endlich! Hast du was vor am Wochenende? Finally! Any plans for the weekend?
You
Ich fahre mit Freunden an den Strand. Und du? I'm going to the beach with some friends. And you?
Neighbour
Nichts Besonderes, einfach zu Hause entspannen. Nothing special, just relaxing at home.
You
Klingt gut! Bis bald! Sounds good! See you around!

Good to know

💡 These phrases use the friendly "du". With older neighbours or in formal settings, use "Sie": "Wie geht es Ihnen?" The weather is a safe topic everywhere.
