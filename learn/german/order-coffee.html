How to order coffee in German

Ordering a coffee is one of the first real conversations you'll have abroad. It's short and predictable, and a great place to build confidence. Here are the phrases you'll actually hear and say at the counter.

Key German phrases

GermanEnglishListen
Hallo / Guten Morgen Hello / Good morning
Ich hätte gern einen Kaffee, bitte. I'd like a coffee, please.
Einen Milchkaffee, bitte. A milky coffee (latte), please.
klein / mittel / groß Small / medium / large
Zum Hiertrinken oder zum Mitnehmen? For here or to go?
Zum Mitnehmen, bitte. To go, please.
Mit Hafermilch, bitte. With oat milk, please.
Was macht das? How much is it?
Kann ich mit Karte zahlen? Can I pay by card?
Danke, schönen Tag noch! Thanks, have a nice day!

Example conversation

Barista
Hallo! Was darf's sein? Hi! What can I get you?
You
Ich hätte gern einen Milchkaffee, bitte. I'd like a latte, please.
Barista
Klein oder groß? Small or large?
You
Groß, zum Mitnehmen. Large, to go.
Barista
Das macht vier Euro fünfzig. That's four euros fifty.
You
Bitte schön. Danke! Here you go. Thanks!

Good to know

💡 "Ich hätte gern…" is the polite, go-to way to order anything. "Was macht das?" (literally "what does that make?") is the everyday way to ask for the total.
