How to make a doctor's appointment in Italian

Calling a doctor's office is stressful in any language, especially on the phone. These phrases help you book the appointment and describe what's wrong clearly.

Key Italian phrases

ItalianEnglishListen
Vorrei prendere un appuntamento. I'd like to make an appointment.
Non mi sento bene. I don't feel well.
Ho la febbre. I have a fever.
Mi fa male la gola. My throat hurts.
Da ieri Since yesterday
C'è disponibilità per domani? Do you have anything available tomorrow?
Di mattina / di pomeriggio In the morning / afternoon
È urgente? Is it urgent?
Sono allergico alla penicillina. I'm allergic to penicillin.
Mi serve la ricetta? Do I need a prescription?

Example conversation

Receptionist
Buongiorno, studio del dottor Rossi. Good morning, Dr. Rossi's office.
You
Buongiorno, vorrei prendere un appuntamento. Hello, I'd like to make an appointment.
Receptionist
Certo. Qual è il problema? Of course. What's the problem?
You
Ho la febbre e mal di gola da ieri. I've had a fever and a sore throat since yesterday.
Receptionist
Abbiamo un posto libero domani alle nove. We have an opening tomorrow at nine.
You
Perfetto, grazie. Perfect, thank you.

Good to know

💡 "Mi fa male…" means "…hurts": "mi fa male la testa" (my head hurts). In Italy a "ricetta" is a prescription as well as a recipe.
