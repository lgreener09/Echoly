How to ask for directions in Italian

Asking for directions is easy. Understanding the answer is the hard part. These phrases cover both sides, plus the one phrase every learner needs: "Could you say that more slowly?"

Key Italian phrases

ItalianEnglishListen
Mi scusi Excuse me
Dov'è la stazione? Where is the train station?
Come arrivo a…? How do I get to…?
È lontano? Is it far?
Vada sempre dritto. Go straight ahead.
Giri a sinistra / a destra. Turn left / right.
Al semaforo At the traffic lights
Può ripetere più lentamente? Could you repeat that more slowly?
Me lo può indicare sulla mappa? Can you show me on the map?
Grazie dell'aiuto! Thanks for your help!

Example conversation

You
Mi scusi, dov'è la stazione? Excuse me, where is the train station?
Local
Vada sempre dritto e al semaforo giri a sinistra. Go straight ahead and turn left at the traffic lights.
You
È lontano? Is it far?
Local
No, circa cinque minuti a piedi. No, about five minutes on foot.
You
Può ripetere più lentamente? Could you repeat that more slowly?
Local
Certo! Dritto, poi a sinistra al semaforo. Of course! Straight, then left at the lights.

Good to know

💡 "Mi scusi" is polite for strangers; "scusa" is for friends. Italians often give directions by landmarks, so listen for phrases like "dopo la chiesa" (after the church).
