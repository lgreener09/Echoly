How to order coffee in Italian

Ordering a coffee is one of the first real conversations you'll have abroad. It's short and predictable, and a great place to build confidence. Here are the phrases you'll actually hear and say at the counter.

Key Italian phrases

ItalianEnglishListen
Ciao / Buongiorno Hello / Good morning
Vorrei un caffè, per favore. I'd like a coffee, please.
Un caffellatte, per favore. A milky coffee (latte), please.
piccolo / medio / grande Small / medium / large
Qui o da portare via? For here or to go?
Da portare via, per favore. To go, please.
Con latte d'avena, per favore. With oat milk, please.
Quant'è? How much is it?
Posso pagare con la carta? Can I pay by card?
Grazie, buona giornata! Thanks, have a nice day!

Example conversation

Barista
Buongiorno! Cosa le preparo? Hi! What can I get you?
You
Vorrei un caffellatte, per favore. I'd like a latte, please.
Barista
Piccolo o grande? Small or large?
You
Grande, da portare via. Large, to go.
Barista
Sono quattro euro e cinquanta. That's four euros fifty.
You
Ecco a lei. Grazie! Here you go. Thanks!

Good to know

💡 In Italy, "un caffè" is an espresso, and ordering just "latte" gets you a glass of milk. Locals often drink coffee standing at the bar, which is usually cheaper than sitting down.
