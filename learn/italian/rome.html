🏛️ Italian for your trip

Italian for Rome: the phrases you'll actually use

Going to Rome? Romans switch to English quickly, but a few words of Italian make every coffee, meal and ticket counter friendlier. These are the phrases you'll use most on a trip to Rome.

Good to know in Rome

💡 “Un caffè” means an espresso. Drinking it standing at the bar (al banco) is usually cheaper than sitting at a table.

💡 A small “coperto” (cover charge) on the restaurant bill is normal in Rome. It isn't a scam.

💡 On buses and trams, remember to validate your ticket in the machine when you get on.
