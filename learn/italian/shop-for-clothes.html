How to shop for clothes in Italian

Sizes, colors, fitting rooms and the all-important "I'll take it". Here's everything you need to shop for clothes without pointing and hoping for the best.

Key Italian phrases

ItalianEnglishListen
Sto solo dando un'occhiata, grazie. I'm just looking, thanks.
Ce l'ha nella taglia M? Do you have this in a medium?
Ce l'ha in un altro colore? Do you have it in another color?
Dove sono i camerini? Where are the fitting rooms?
Posso provarlo? Can I try it on?
È troppo grande / troppo piccolo. It's too big / too small.
Mi sta bene. It fits well.
Quanto costa? How much does it cost?
È in saldo? Is it on sale?
Lo prendo. I'll take it.

Example conversation

Shop assistant
Buongiorno! Posso aiutarla? Hello! Can I help you?
You
Sì, ha questa camicia nella taglia M? Yes, do you have this shirt in a medium?
Shop assistant
Controllo subito... Ecco qua. Let me check... Here you go.
You
Grazie. Dove sono i camerini? Thanks. Where are the fitting rooms?
Shop assistant
In fondo a sinistra. At the back, on the left.
You
Mi sta bene. La prendo! It fits well. I'll take it!

Good to know

💡 "Mi sta bene" means it fits or suits you. Italian clothing sizes use different numbers from the US and UK, so it's worth asking "Che taglia è?" (what size is it?).
