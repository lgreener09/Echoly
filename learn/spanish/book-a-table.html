How to book a table in Spanish

Booking a table means a phone call, which is the scariest kind of conversation because you can't point at anything. These phrases cover the whole call, from the greeting to spelling your name.

Key Spanish phrases

SpanishEnglishListen
Quisiera reservar una mesa. I'd like to book a table.
Para cuatro personas For four people
Esta noche a las ocho Tonight at eight
¿Tienen mesa en la terraza? Do you have a table outside?
A nombre de… Under the name…
¿Hay algo más tarde? Is there anything later?

Tengo que cancelar mi reserva. I need to cancel my reservation.

Example conversation

Restaurant
Restaurante La Piazza, ¿dígame? Hello, La Piazza restaurant.
You
Hola, quisiera reservar una mesa para cuatro para esta noche. Hello, I'd like to book a table for four tonight.
Restaurant
¿A qué hora? What time?
You
A las ocho, si es posible. At eight, if possible.
Restaurant
Perfecto. ¿A nombre de quién? Perfect. Under what name?
You
A nombre de Taylor. ¡Hasta esta noche! Under the name Taylor. See you tonight!

Good to know

💡 Spaniards eat late: dinner bookings at 21:00 or 22:00 are normal, and many kitchens don't open before 20:30. "¿Dígame?" is a common way to answer the phone.
