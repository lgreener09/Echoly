Spanish for Mexico City: the phrases you'll actually use

Heading to Mexico City? A little Spanish goes a long way at taco stands, markets and in taxis, and locals really appreciate the effort. These are the phrases you'll actually use, in the Mexican Spanish you'll hear there.

Good to know in Mexico City

💡 In Mexico, people often say “¿Mande?” when they didn't catch something. It just means “Pardon?”.

💡 At street stands you usually pay at the end. Ask “¿Cuánto le debo?” (How much do I owe you?).

💡 Tipping 10–15% (la propina) is normal at sit-down restaurants.
